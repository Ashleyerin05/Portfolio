Featured Project

StudioKey

StudioKey is a management platform I designed for boutique fitness studios that are stuck overpaying for software built for large chains. By leveraging a modern tech stack and cutting unnecessary features like marketplace listings, it delivers scheduling, analytics, instructor performance tracking, and AI-powered coaching at a fraction of the typical cost. The platform serves three distinct user types through dedicated portals for customers, managers, and instructors, with a pricing model designed to scale alongside the studio's growth.
