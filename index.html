AI ProductWorkflow Design

Making an AI proposal tool buildable

AI Operations & Business Strategy Intern · Intelligence Assist · Sydney

A startup wanted an internal AI proposal generator, but the workflow lived in people's heads — making it impossible to build or scale.

What this shows: I take a vague AI ambition and turn it into a concrete, buildable spec — and I judge AI tools by whether they move a real outcome.

Insight
You can't automate a process you haven't made explicit. The bottleneck was clarity, not technology.
What I did
Mapped the end-to-end logic flow for the proposal generator, then evaluated and recommended AI tools that integrated directly into client strategy sessions. Standardized output with markdown scripting for consistent branding.
Outcome
Reduced the complexity of future tool development and shipped a repeatable, on-brand proposal process into live client work.
Product Strategy0→1 Prototype

StudioKey — software boutique studios can actually afford

Self-initiated product · Concept & prototype

Boutique fitness studios overpay for management software built for large chains — bloated with features they'll never use.

What this shows: Product instinct — I scope by cutting, segment users deliberately, and treat pricing as part of the product.

Insight
Small studios don't need a marketplace or enterprise sprawl. They need scheduling, analytics, and coaching — priced to their scale.
What I did
Designed a focused platform with three dedicated portals (customer, manager, instructor), AI-powered coaching, and instructor-performance analytics. Deliberately cut enterprise features to lower cost, with a pricing model that scales with studio growth.
Outcome
A scoped, prototyped product concept delivering the core jobs studios pay for — at a fraction of incumbent cost.
