About

I'm a rising senior at Boston University's Questrom School of Business, studying Information Systems and Artificial Intelligence. My interest in product started with a simple realization: the systems people rely on every day are only as good as the thinking behind them.

I've gotten to build across a lot of different problems — architecting wholesale pricing and quoting tools, prototyping a B2B ordering platform, mapping AI tool workflows at a Sydney startup, and designing an end-to-end product strategy from 17 user interviews.

What ties it all together is a belief that technology should serve people — turning problems into scalable solutions that resonate with users and drive real business value. Outside of work, I teach yoga, cook constantly, and love finding calm in a busy world.
